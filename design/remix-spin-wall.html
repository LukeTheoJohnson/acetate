What this spike tests

  • The honest question: does 4-deck layering hold up musically, or does it turn to mud — and do the FOCUS kills (one record on LOWS, another on TOPS) rescue it, classic EQ-mixing style?
  • Where the practical ceiling sits: is the sweet spot two full records plus two killed ones, or can four coexist at reduced gains?
  • Tempo-lock only fixes time — records in clashing keys will still fight harmonically. Does that matter in practice, or does the drum-heavy material shrug it off?
  • Gain staging: four beds default to 0.70 each — is that enough headroom, or does the wall need an automatic gain share (1/√n) per live deck?
  • One caveat baked in: the master sweep chains a second .lpf() after a LOWS kill's .lpf(400); if Strudel's control semantics let the later value win, the sweep temporarily opens a killed deck back up. Worth probing against the CDN bundle before productising.
