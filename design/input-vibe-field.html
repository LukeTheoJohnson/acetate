Why this is clearer

One gesture — drag the puck — simultaneously sets energy and mood, eliminating the conceptual overlap between the old pad, per-part faders, and density slider. The field is a named emotional territory: you navigate to a vibe, not a pair of numbers.

The trade-off: per-part emphasis (kick/bass/lead etc.) is dropped from the primary gesture. Density is demoted to a single secondary slider. Genre is a quick chip selection that tints the field's landmark palette, keeping visual context without competing with the main control.
