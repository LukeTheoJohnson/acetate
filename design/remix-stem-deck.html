What this spike tests

  • Whether colour-tagged stack lines from render() can be reliably split into DRUMS / BASS / CHORDS / LEAD / AIR stem groups without touching the engine — a pure client-side parse.
  • Whether mute/solo controls per stem group, applied at compose time by filtering which layers enter the recomposed stack(), give the mixing depth of a hardware stem mixer for free.
  • Whether the one-touch swap macros ("A drums × B melody") produce an instantly compelling mashup in a single click — the core Stem Deck thesis.
  • Whether records that cannot be split (arranged or remix pressings with no colour tags) fall back gracefully to an unsplittable FULL block without breaking the composition path.
  • Whether PRESS on a stem-sculpted mix produces a crate record that is itself splittable on reload (it will be, since the surviving colour-tagged lines are preserved in the composed program).
