Why this is clearer

A Kaoss-style XY pad collapses two continuous parameters — energy and mood — into a single gesture you can play rather than dial in. Your muscle memory builds up naturally: push into the top-right corner to drive a bright, maximal drop; pull to the bottom-left to strip back into a dark, stripped groove. The glowing trail makes performed movement legible — a reviewer can see the arc of your intention, not just a final position.

The trade-off is discoverability: the axes and their poles need clear labelling, and users unfamiliar with Kaoss pads may initially treat the pad as a button rather than an instrument. The latch affordance mitigates the "hands-off" problem, and the genre strip recontextualises the pad's colour palette so the same gesture feels different per genre.
