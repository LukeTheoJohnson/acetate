Why this is clearer
Collapsing energy + density into one Intensity axis removes the most common source of confusion — both controls pulled in the same direction. The part mixer replaces seven anonymous faders with an explicit three-state choice (out / auto / feature), making the DJ's latitude visible rather than hidden. Trade-off: merging two axes loses independent control — you can't have a hard-hitting but sparse arrangement, though that edge case is rare enough that the gained clarity outweighs it.
