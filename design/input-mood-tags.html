Why this is clearer

Instead of abstract faders — a crowd pad, a density slider, seven per-part knobs — this paradigm lets you describe the track you want the way you'd describe it to a friend: "something hazy and laid-back, Lo-Fi, sparse, with the chords out front." Every word maps to the same numeric parameters under the hood, but the mental model is intent rather than engineering. The trade-off is precision: you cannot set energy to exactly +0.37, but that granularity rarely matters when the AI is interpreting mood — the word "driving" is faster to reach and easier to remember than a half-pushed fader.
