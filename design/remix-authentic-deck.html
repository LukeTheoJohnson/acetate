What this deck is

  • One idea: the phrase is the clock. The 32-bar timeline drives everything — ARM a transition and it fires on the bar, not whenever.
  • Stems are exact, not AI: because this app composed the record, it can split it for free. Muting BASS on a deck IS the bass-swap — no approximation, no fake EQ.
  • Filter per deck: centre = open; left (negative) → LP sweep down to ~200 Hz; right (positive) → HP sweep up to ~3.2 kHz. Single knob, no fake 3-band.
  • Hold-to-fire paddles: STUTTER (.ply("4")), GATE (.struct("1 0 1 0 1 0 1 0")), ECHO-OUT (.delay(0.4).delaytime(0.1875).delayfeedback(0.6).room(0.3)) — lifted on mouseup, no lingering state.
  • Deferred: solo-cue, loop-roll, reload, spinback.
