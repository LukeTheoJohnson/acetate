What this spike tests

  • Whether a phrase clock driven by Date.now() is close enough to the Strudel scheduler for practical mix decisions — minor drift is acknowledged and noted.
  • Whether ARM → boundary quantisation (8 / 16 / 32 bars) gives a real DJ's confidence over the "fader whenever" approach — the drop should land on the grid, not between ticks.
  • Whether auto-fade (ramping the crossfader across the bars before a boundary, throttled ~150ms re-evaluates) lands cleanly on the phrase line without a perceptible stutter.
  • Whether shrinking the disc to a small identity badge and making the 32-bar timeline the primary visual improves spatial legibility over the twin-platter layout.
  • Whether the MIX NOW pulse light (last 1 bar before each boundary) creates enough urgency to encourage well-timed transitions without being distracting mid-mix.
  • Known limitation: the phrase clock accumulates small drift vs the Web Audio scheduler because Date.now() does not sync to Strudel's internal cycle counter. For a spike this is acceptable; a production version would hook into the engine's cycle event.
