    Simulation controls

    Auto-run lets the hidden listener react on its own (with noise + the odd contrarian tap) so you can watch convergence hands-free. Step plays one window using your last feedback. Manual buttons override the sim — that's you being the listener. Try switching persona and watching Q re-home.
    What you're seeing. Update rule: Q[a] ← Q[a] + α·(r − Q[a]), α = 0.15, optimistic init. Selection: ε-greedy, ε decays 0.5·e^(−t/12) with a 0.12 floor + surprise re-trigger. Reward r fuses explicit taps (±1 / −0.6 skip) with a passive dwell channel. The listener's true preference is hidden from the learner — exactly the setup on a real floor. Every ~8 windows the DJ injects a deliberate hold-out probe (its worst-rated arm) and checks the reward gap: that's the within-session test for "is it modelling me, or regressing to a bland mean?".
